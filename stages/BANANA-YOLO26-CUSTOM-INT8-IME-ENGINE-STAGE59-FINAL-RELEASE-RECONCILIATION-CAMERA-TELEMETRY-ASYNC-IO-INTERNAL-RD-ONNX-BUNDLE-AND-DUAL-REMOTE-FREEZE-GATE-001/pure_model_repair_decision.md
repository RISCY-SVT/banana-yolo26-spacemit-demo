# Pure-Model Repair Decision

**Selected:** make the accepted compiler contract intrinsic to the official
release object target and require the fail-closed official K1X CMake option.

**Rejected:** arithmetic changes, model/package changes, new kernel routes, or
benchmark-specific environment compensation.

The final same-session randomized ABBA used 1,000 samples per arm after 30
warmups. Stage57 measured 133356.369 us total mean and 134949.350 us p95;
Stage59 measured 134100.921 us total mean and 135724.150 us p95. The Stage59
mean is 0.558% above the rebuilt Stage57 control and therefore satisfies the
required within-1% equivalence gate as well as the preferred absolute 135500 us
mean and 137000 us p95 limits. The corresponding internal executor means were
130699.600 and 131422.434 us, a 0.553% delta. Every sample produced
`0xd43f5e018b415631`, affinity failures were zero, CPU4-7 IME count was zero,
and the O2 wrapper restored its snapshot, cgroup, and workqueue state.
