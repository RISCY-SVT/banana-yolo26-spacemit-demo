## Candidate topology

Measure before selecting one of:

- fixed telephoto;
- wide plus telephoto;
- wide high-resolution plus ROI crop;
- PTZ.

No topology is selected by Stage65D. COCO small/medium/large are pixel-area
bins, not physical species sizes.
