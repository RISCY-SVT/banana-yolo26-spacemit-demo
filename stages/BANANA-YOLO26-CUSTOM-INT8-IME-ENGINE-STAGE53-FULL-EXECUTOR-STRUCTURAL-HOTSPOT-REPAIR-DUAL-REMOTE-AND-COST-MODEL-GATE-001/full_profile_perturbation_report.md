# Profile perturbation

The selected epoch-spin route was measured for 20 warmups and 50 retained complete profile runs. A timer surrounds each operation or resident range; no element-level instrumentation is present.

All 215 manifest operations are represented by 208 timed execution ranges. Fused activation/Add work and view-only Split operations share a producer/resident timer rather than receiving an invented independent duration.

Uninstrumented mean was 239884.016000 us and profiled outer mean was 240287.166920 us, for +0.168061% perturbation. Mean operation sum was 239771.361700 us (99.785338% of outer wall).
